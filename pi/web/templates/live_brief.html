JAFO listens to public-safety radio with its own receivers. Speech-to-text writes down each call, and an AI writes it in plain English and groups calls into stories. Locations are rounded to the block, private details are hidden, and transcripts can be wrong. Encrypted channels aren't heard. This is not an emergency service: call 911 if you need help.
