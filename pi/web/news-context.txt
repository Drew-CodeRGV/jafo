### Traffic / MVA / Crashes / Pursuits
- Texas recorded 4,150 traffic deaths in 2024, about 11 per day — roughly one
  every two hours and seven minutes. (TxDOT Motor Vehicle Traffic Crash Facts, 2024)
- A reportable traffic crash occurred somewhere in Texas every 57 seconds in
  2024, and there was not a single deathless day on Texas roads. (TxDOT, 2024)
- Hidalgo County is one of the most populous and highest-traffic counties in
  South Texas; Expressway 83 (I-2) and Expressway 281 (I-69C/E) are its busiest
  corridors. (general RGV geography)

### Weather / Heat (also useful as ambient color any time of year)
- McAllen summers are sweltering: July and August average highs sit in the
  mid-90s to near 99°F. (NWS / climate normals)
- The Rio Grande Valley is semi-arid, averaging roughly 20 inches of rain a
  year, with September typically the wettest month. (climate normals)
- Heat is a year-round factor in the Valley; first responders routinely work
  calls in high heat and humidity from spring through fall. (climate normals)

### Standing area color (always true — safe for any tone)
- McAllen anchors the Hidalgo County metro of roughly 870,000 residents; the
  greater Rio Grande Valley is home to well over a million people along the
  Texas–Mexico border. (US Census / regional estimates — see rgv-context.txt)
