Notes. Why two RTL-SDRs, not an Airspy: two $40 dongles give every student both halves of JAFO (P25 on one, the sky on the other) and teach more: per-dongle serials, SDR windows, and a smart time-share. The trade-off vs. the Airspy's wide window is fewer simultaneous voice captures on a very busy system; fine for a workshop and most cities. Production reference: the RGV node runs an Airspy R2 on P25 plus one RTL-SDR shared between ADS-B and ACARS. Sources: Pi 5: PiShop/Adafruit/CanaKit · RTL-SDR V4: rtl-sdr.com · Airspy: airspy.us · SDRplay: sdrplay.com. 3D case: we supply the STL (fits Pi 5 + 2× V4 + fan, with antenna/USB cut-outs); ~$4 in filament self-printed, ~$15–25 printed to order. Bulk: expect modest per-unit savings ordering 20+ seats.
